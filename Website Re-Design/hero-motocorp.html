Hero MotoCorp · Grey India

Happy Earth

700,000+ Trees Planted Across India Strategist & Copywriter — Digital & Content

A CSR campaign that planted 700,000 trees — one name at a time.

How do you get millions of people to care about the environment? You give them the simplest possible act of participation — and make the brand carry the weight.

The Brief

How do you engage users in a CSR activity online, for India's leading two-wheeler company? Hero MotoCorp wanted to demonstrate its commitment to the environment in a way that was participatory, not just declarative.

The Pivot

The brief was simple: build a CSR page. Something to sit quietly on the Hero MotoCorp website and tick the box. We looked at what they were actually doing — planting trees across India, on behalf of anyone who asked — and said this is too big for a page. This needs a world of its own. So we built it one.

The Execution

The Happy Earth microsite launched on World Environment Day 2016. It sensitised users about the environmental concerns facing the planet and Hero MotoCorp's initiatives to address them. Users could participate by sharing their name and email — Hero MotoCorp would plant a tree on their behalf. Simple, tangible, scalable.

"The most powerful CSR isn't a statement. It's an invitation — one where the brand does the heavy lifting and the user gets to feel the impact."

The Solution

A digital-first CSR platform that transformed passive environmental awareness into active participation — at a scale that made the impact real and measurable.
